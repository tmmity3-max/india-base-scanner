How the screens work

Trend Template
Price above the 50, 150 and 200-day averages in that order, the 200-day rising for a month, at least 25% above the 52-week low, within 25% of the 52-week high, and RS rating of 70 or more.
VCP
Passes the Trend Template, then at least two pullbacks in the last six months, each no deeper than the one before, the last under 12%, volume drying up, and price within 6% of the pivot (the high of the last pullback).
Blue Sky
Within 3% of the all-time high, with at least three years of history and RS of 70 or more. No old buyers waiting to sell above.
Multi-Year Base
The highest price was set two or more years ago and has not been beaten since. Price is now near or just above that old high.
IPO Base
Listed within the last two years, has built a base at least 10 days long and no deeper than 35%, and is now within 8% of its post-listing high.
RS rating
Weighted 12-month return, with the latest quarter counting double, ranked 1–99 against every stock scanned.
